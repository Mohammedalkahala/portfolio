About me

I'm Mohammed Alkahala, a security operations analyst working toward detection engineering and threat hunting. I work in the SOC by day and build, test and break detections in my home lab in the evenings.

My open-source project, PurpleLoop, is built on that lab. The rule is simple: every detection ships with proof. I reproduce the attack safely, write Wazuh and Sigma rules, run the test, try to bypass my own rules, and publish the results, including what failed and why.

The lab

  • Proxmox host with OPNsense segmenting LAN, attack and SOC networks
  • Active Directory domain controller, Windows 11 workstations, Linux servers and Kali
  • Sysmon on Windows endpoints and a Wazuh agent on every machine
  • AI agents that draft rules, grade them, and manage lab infrastructure with read-only defaults and approval for every change, while Wazuh watches the agents themselves

Background

  • B.S. in Cybersecurity, Eastern Michigan University
  • Co-author of an EISA 2025 conference paper on the attack surface of the Canvas learning management system
  • CompTIA Security+, CySA+, Linux+
  • English and Arabic
